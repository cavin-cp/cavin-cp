## What I'm building

[JobsPaid](https://jobspaid.ai) turns a voice note, photo, or text on WhatsApp into a professional invoice or quote in 30 seconds. A plumber finishes a job, sends one voice note on WhatsApp, and 30 seconds later the customer has a clean invoice with payment links — before he leaves the driveway.

Live in **10 countries**: US, Brazil, Mexico, India, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Colombia, Chile, Argentina.

The application repository is private. Happy to grant read access for diligence on request.
